Wave Relationship & Formulas

The electron moves along the wave group with velocity vg, while the individual crests of the wave move with phase velocity vp.

- vp = ω / k → velocity of wave crests.
- vg = dω / dk → velocity of energy/envelope.
- Increasing wave speed causes the wave crests to move ahead of the electron, showing the physical separation between phase and group motion.
